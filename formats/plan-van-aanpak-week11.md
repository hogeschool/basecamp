## Format Plan van Aanpak (week 11)

[English scroll down]

> Plan van Aanpak Bij de zelfevaluatie heb je gekeken naar hoe je de afgelopen weken hebt gewerkt; wat ging er goed en wat kan er beter? Ook heb je je eerder opgestelde leerdoelen geëvalueerd; wat is er gelukt en wat niet en waarom? Daarnaast heb je feedback gekregen van jouw docenten. Nu gaan we naar de toekomst kijken; waar moet je bijsturen en wat wil je de komende tijd bereiken en hoe ga je dat doen? Je gaat hiervoor weer persoonlijke leerdoelen formuleren. Deze zijn gericht op kennis en vaardigheden die jij wil ontwikkelen, waar bijsturing bij nodig is en die nodig zijn om jouw studie tot een succes te maken. De inzichten uit de zelfevaluatie en feedbacksessie helpen je hierbij.

## Format Plan of Approach (week 6)

> Plan of approach During the self-evaluation, you looked at how you worked over the past few weeks; what went well and what could be improved? You also evaluated your previously established learning goals; what succeeded and what didn't and why? In addition, you have received feedback from your teachers. Now we are going to look to the future; where do you need to adjust and what do you want to achieve in the near future and how are you going to do that? You will formulate personal learning goals. These are focused on knowledge and skills that you want to develop, where adjustments are needed and which are necessary to make your study a success. The insights from the self-evaluation and feedback session will help you with this.

## Looking forward – plan of approach

Now we look ahead: where do you need to make adjustments, what do you want to achieve in the coming period and how are you going to do that? To give yourself direction here, you will again formulate personal learning goals in Arch 3.

It may be that your self-evaluation or the feedback session has shown that you still need to work on your previously set learning goals. If so, consider what concrete actions are needed to get started on the learning goal in the right way. You can, of course, also formulate new learning goals. Here, too, you will link concrete actions to them. You are going to challenge yourself. What do you want to work on or pay attention to? How are you going to tackle this?

Answer the questions below. Take the time to formulate your answers carefully and substantiate them. Short or superficial answers do not provide sufficient insight into your learning process and development.
