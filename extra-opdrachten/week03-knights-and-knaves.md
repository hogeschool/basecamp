These questions concern fictitious people, each of whom either always lies or always speaks the truth.  In each case, write a Python program that finds out who is lying and who is telling the truth.  [Hint: assign boolean values: True for truth tellers, and False for liars.  Loop over all possibilities, and figure out how to check consistency with the given statements]

**Question 1:**

Andrej says: Babak is a liar.

Babak says: Cigdem is a liar.

Cigdem says: Andrej and Babak are both liars.

​

**Question 2:**

Andrej says: if Babak and I both speak the truth, then Cigdem is a liar.

Babak says: Andrej speaks the truth.

Cigdem says: I speak the truth.

​

**Question 3:**

Andrej says: if Cigdem is a liar, then Babak is also a liar.

Babak says: I speak the truth.

Cigdem says: if Andrej speaks the truth, then Babak is a liar.

​

**Question 4:**

Everybody plays either football or rugby.  In one of these groups, everybody always speaks the truth, and in the other group, everyone always lies.

Andrej says: Babak plays rugby.

Babak says: Cigdem plays football.

Cigdem says: Andrej plays football, and Babak plays rugby.
