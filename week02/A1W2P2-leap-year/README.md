Most years have 365 days. However, the time required for the Earth to orbit the Sun is actually slightly more than that. <br>
As a result, an extra day, February 29, is included in some years to correct for this difference. Such years are referred to as leap years. <br>
Write a program that asks a year from the user and then displays a message indicating whether or not it is a leap year.

###### Criteria:
- Any year that is divisible by 400 is a leap year.
- Of the remaining years, any year that is divisible by 100 is not a leap year.
- Of the remaining years, any year that is divisible by 4 is a leap year.
- All other years are not leap years.

###### Input examples:
1. `2012`
2. `2011`

###### Output examples:
1. `Leap year`
2. `Not a leap year`
